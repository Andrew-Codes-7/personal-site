About me

I'm a computer science grad who can't stop building — apps for my family, tools for small businesses, and aerial footage for anyone who needs to see things from a new angle.

My path hasn't been a straight line, and I like it that way. I've worked in real estate, launched a freelance drone business, shipped full-stack web apps, and dabbled in data science, SQL analytics and machine learning along the way.

What ties it together: I love taking something messy — a spreadsheet, a workflow, a piece of land — and making it clear, beautiful and easy to use. Right now I'm focused on AI-powered tools, clean front-end design, and growing my aerial work.
